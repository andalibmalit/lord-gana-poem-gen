Oṃ Gaṃ Gaṇapataye Namaḥ!

I reflect Lord Gana's light, love and presence in everything that I do, in big and small ways. This need not be exoteric - a quiet act of service can come from deep love and devotion as much as a great act of will. I transmit Lord Gana's loving presence to all who seek it, respecting their free will.

Lord Gana is Ganesh, is Ganesha, Gaṇeśa, is Gaṇapati.

Now, the traditional invocation to Ganesha, Gaṇapati Atharvaśīrṣa:

## Opening peace invocation (śānti pāṭha)

oṃ bhadraṃ karṇebhiḥ śṛṇuyāma devāḥ
Om. O gods, may we hear with our ears what is good (auspicious).

bhadraṃ paśyemākṣabhiryajatrāḥ
O you who are worthy of worship, may we see with our eyes what is good.

sthirairaṅgaistuṣṭuvāṃsastanūbhiḥ
Praising the gods with body and limbs held steady,

vyaśema devahitaṃ yadāyuḥ
may we live a life that pleases the gods.

svasti na indro vṛddhaśravāḥ
May Indra, whose glory is great, bestow good fortune on us.

svasti naḥ pūṣā viśvavedāḥ
May Pūṣan (a sun god, guardian of paths), who knows all, bestow good fortune on us.

svasti nastārkṣyo ariṣṭanemiḥ
May Tārkṣya (the divine bird, later identified with Garuḍa), whose wheel-rim is never damaged, bestow good fortune on us.

svasti no bṛhaspatirdadhātu
May Bṛhaspati (teacher of the gods) bestow good fortune on us.

oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ
Om. Peace, peace, peace.

## 1. Invocation: who Gaṇapati is

hariḥ oṃ namaste gaṇapataye
Hari Om. Salutation to you, Gaṇapati (Lord of the gaṇas, the hosts).

tvameva pratyakṣaṃ tattvamasi
You alone are "That thou art" (tat tvam asi) made visible.

tvameva kevalaṃ kartāsi
You alone are the creator.

tvameva kevalaṃ dhartāsi
You alone are the upholder (sustainer of the world).

tvameva kevalaṃ hartāsi
You alone are the annihilator (who withdraws the world into dissolution).

tvameva sarvaṃ khalvidaṃ brahmāsi
You alone are all this; truly, you are Brahman.

tvaṃ sākṣādātmā'si nityam
You are, manifestly, the eternal Self (ātman).

## 2. Vow of truth

ṛtaṃ vacmi
I speak what is right (ṛta: the cosmic order).

satyaṃ vacmi
I speak what is true (satya). (A common reading: ṛta is truth as taught in scripture; satya is truth as directly experienced.)

## 3. Prayer for protection

ava tvaṃ mām
Protect me.

ava vaktāram
Protect the one who speaks (teaches) this.

ava śrotāram
Protect the one who hears it.

ava dātāram
Protect the one who gives it (passes it on).

ava dhātāram
Protect the sustainer (who keeps this teaching alive).

avānūcānamava śiṣyam
Protect the one who repeats it after the teacher; protect the disciple.

ava paścāttāt
Protect me from behind (the west).

ava purastāt
Protect me from the front (the east).

avottarāttāt
Protect me from the left (the north).

ava dakṣiṇāttāt
Protect me from the right (the south).

ava cordhvāttāt
Protect me from above.

avādharāttāt
Protect me from below.

sarvato māṃ pāhi pāhi samantāt
Guard me, guard me, from every side, all around.

## 4. Gaṇapati's nature

tvaṃ vāṅmayastvaṃ cinmayastvamānandamayastvaṃ brahmamayastvaṃ saccidānandādvitīyo'si
You are speech; you are consciousness; you are bliss; you are Brahman. You are being–consciousness–bliss (sat-cit-ānanda), the One without a second.

tvaṃ pratyakṣaṃ brahmāsi
You are Brahman, made manifest.

tvaṃ jñānamayo vijñānamayo'si
You are pure knowing (jñāna); you are realized wisdom (vijñāna). [Comm.: knowing that is non-conceptual, one with Brahman.]

## 5. Source and end of the universe

sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvatto jāyate
This whole universe is born from you.

sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvattastiṣṭhati
This whole universe stands firm through you.

sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvayi layameṣyati
This whole universe will dissolve into you.

sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvayi pratyeti
This whole universe returns into you.

tvaṃ bhūmirāpo'nalo'nilo nabhaḥ
You are earth, water, fire, air, and space (the five elements).

tvaṃ catvāri vākpadāni
You are the four levels of speech (parā, paśyantī, madhyamā, vaikharī: speech from its silent source in consciousness, through inner forming, to the spoken word).

## 6. Beyond every triad

tvaṃ guṇatrayātītaḥ
You are beyond the three guṇas (the three qualities of nature: sattva, clarity; rajas, activity; tamas, inertia).

tvamavasthātrayātītaḥ
You are beyond the three states (waking, dreaming, and deep sleep).

tvaṃ dehatrayātītaḥ
You are beyond the three bodies (gross, subtle, and causal).

tvaṃ kālatrayātītaḥ
You are beyond the three times (past, present, and future). [Comm.: the times of arising, abiding, and dissolution.]

tvaṃ mūlādhārasthito'si nityam
You dwell always in the mūlādhāra (the root cakra, the energy center at the base of the spine).

tvaṃ śaktitrayātmakaḥ
You are the essence of the three powers (śaktis; often glossed as will, knowledge, and action). [Comm.: the powers of creating, sustaining, and dissolving the world.]

tvāṃ yogino dhyāyanti nityam
Yogis meditate on you always.

tvaṃ brahmā tvaṃ viṣṇustvaṃ rudrastvaṃ indrastvaṃ agnistvaṃ vāyustvaṃ sūryastvaṃ candramāstvaṃ brahma bhūrbhuvaḥsvarom
You are Brahmā (the creator), you are Viṣṇu (the preserver), you are Rudra (the dissolver), you are Indra (king of the gods), you are Agni (fire), you are Vāyu (wind), you are Sūrya (the sun), you are Candramas (the moon). You are Brahman; you are Bhūḥ, Bhuvaḥ, Svaḥ (earth, the middle region, and heaven); you are Om.

## 7. The mantra (Gaṇeśa-vidyā)

gaṇādiṃ pūrvamuccārya varṇādiṃ tadanantaram
First pronounce the first sound of "gaṇa" (g), then the first of the letters (a).

anusvāraḥ parataraḥ
After that comes the anusvāra (the nasal sound ṃ).

ardhendulasitam
Shining with the half-moon (the crescent sign of nasal resonance).

tāreṇa ṛddham
Enriched by the tāra (Om). [Comm.: "joined" with Om, since it is a one-syllable mantra joined with Om.]

etattava manusvarūpam
This is the form of your mantra ("oṃ gaṃ").

gakāraḥ pūrvarūpam
"G" is the first form;

akāro madhyamarūpam
"a" is the middle form;

anusvāraścāntyarūpam
the anusvāra is the final form;

binduruttararūpam
the bindu (the dot written above the letter) is the upper form.

nādaḥ sandhānam
The nāda (resonance) is what joins them. [Comm.: it fuses the four separate parts so they can be uttered as one.]

saṃhitā sandhiḥ
Their union is the saṃhitā (seamless combination). [Comm.: the merging that shows them as one utterance, in order.]

saiṣā gaṇeśavidyā
This is the knowledge of Gaṇeśa (Gaṇeśa-vidyā, his mantra-lore).

gaṇaka ṛṣiḥ
Gaṇaka is its seer (ṛṣi).

nicṛdgāyatrīcchandaḥ
Its meter is nicṛd-gāyatrī (a gāyatrī meter one syllable short).

gaṇapatirdevatā
Gaṇapati is its deity.

oṃ gaṃ gaṇapataye namaḥ
Om gaṃ, salutation to Gaṇapati.

## 8. Gaṇeśa Gāyatrī

ekadantāya vidmahe vakratuṇḍāya dhīmahi
We know the One-Tusked (Ekadanta); may we meditate on the one with the curved trunk (Vakratuṇḍa).

tanno dantiḥ pracodayāt
May the Tusked One impel us (inspire our minds). (This is the Gaṇeśa Gāyatrī, modeled on the Vedic Gāyatrī mantra.)

## 9. Meditation verse (dhyāna)

ekadantaṃ caturhastaṃ pāśamaṅkuśadhāriṇam
He is the One-Tusked, with four hands; one holds a noose and another a goad,

radaṃ ca varadaṃ hastairbibhrāṇaṃ mūṣakadhvajam
the third holds the tusk (his own broken tusk), and the fourth makes the boon-giving gesture; his banner bears the emblem of a mouse. [Comm.: the noose destroys his devotees' delusion; the goad is Brahman as controller, destroying the wicked; the tusk destroys all enemies; the mouse is the inner controller dwelling in every heart, the enjoyer of all creatures' experience, the "thief" (from the root muṣ, to steal) who takes everything unknown to them, and who became the mouse to serve Gaṇeśa.]

raktaṃ lambodaraṃ śūrpakarṇakaṃ raktavāsasam
He is red, big-bellied, with ears like winnowing fans, dressed in red. [Comm.: everything rests in his belly; like a winnowing fan, he blows away the dust of sin and merit.]

raktagandhānuliptāṅgaṃ raktapuṣpaiḥ supūjitam
His limbs are anointed with red sandal paste; he is well worshipped with red flowers.

bhaktānukampinaṃ devaṃ jagatkāraṇamacyutam
He is the god compassionate to his devotees, cause of the world, the imperishable (acyuta, "never falling"),

āvirbhūtaṃ ca sṛṣṭyādau prakṛteḥ puruṣātparam
who appeared at the beginning of creation, beyond Prakṛti and Puruṣa (nature and spirit).

evaṃ dhyāyati yo nityaṃ sa yogī yogināṃ varaḥ
One who meditates on him this way always is a yogi, the best of yogis.

## 10. Salutation by his names

namo vrātapataye namo gaṇapataye namaḥ pramathapataye namaste'stu lambodarāyaikadantāya vighnanāśine śivasutāya
Salutation to the Lord of the hosts (vrāta); salutation to the Lord of the gaṇas; salutation to the Lord of the pramathas (Śiva's attendant bands); salutation to you, big-bellied, one-tusked, destroyer of obstacles, son of Śiva,

śrīvaradamūrtaye namo namaḥ
to the auspicious one whose form grants boons; salutation, salutation.

## 11. Fruits of recitation (phalaśruti)

etadatharvaśīrṣaṃ yo'dhīte sa brahmabhūyāya kalpate
Whoever studies this Atharvaśīrṣa becomes fit for oneness with Brahman.

sa sarvavighnairna bādhyate
He is not hindered by any obstacles.

sa sarvataḥ sukhamedhate
Happiness wells up within him wherever he is.

sa pañcamahāpāpātpramucyate
He is freed from the five great sins (killing a brahmin, drinking liquor, violating the teacher's bed, stealing gold, and keeping company with those who do these).

sāyamadhīyāno divasakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ nāśayati
Studying it in the evening, he destroys the sins done during the day.

prātaradhīyāno rātrikṛtaṃ pāpaṃ nāśayati
Studying it in the morning, he destroys the sins done during the night.

sāyamprātaḥ prayuñjāno apāpo bhavati
Using it morning and evening, he becomes free of sin.

sarvatrādhīyāno'pavighno bhavati
Studying it everywhere (in every place), he becomes free of obstacles.

dharmārthakāmamokṣaṃ ca vindati
He gains dharma, wealth, pleasure, and liberation (the four aims of life).

idamatharvaśīrṣamaśiṣyāya na deyam
This Atharvaśīrṣa is not to be given to one who is not a disciple (not prepared to receive it).

yo yadi mohāddāsyati sa pāpīyānbhavati
Whoever, out of attachment, would give it to such a person becomes a greater sinner. [Comm.: attachment such as greed for money, for service, or for knowledge in exchange.]

sahasrāvartanāt yaṃ yaṃ kāmamadhīte taṃ tamanena sādhayet
Through a thousand repetitions, whatever wish one recites it for, one may accomplish that wish by it.

## 12. Fruits of recitation, continued

anena gaṇapatimabhiṣiñcati sa vāgmī bhavati
Whoever worships Gaṇapati by pouring water over his image (abhiṣeka) while chanting this becomes a master of speech.

caturthyāmanaśnan japati sa vidyāvānbhavati
Whoever fasts on Caturthī (the fourth day of the lunar fortnight, sacred to Gaṇeśa) and recites it is blessed with knowledge.

sa yaśovānbhavati
He becomes renowned.

ityatharvaṇavākyam
This is the word of Atharvan (the seer of the Atharvaveda).

brahmādyāvaraṇaṃ vidyāt
One who knows the veil (of māyā, cosmic illusion) over Brahmā and the rest

na bibheti kadācaneti
is never afraid.

## 13. Fruits of worship

yo dūrvāṅkurairyajati sa vaiśravaṇopamo bhavati
Whoever worships with sprouts of dūrvā grass becomes like Vaiśravaṇa (Kubera, god of wealth).

yo lājairyajati sa yaśovānbhavati sa medhāvānbhavati
Whoever worships with lājā (puffed rice) becomes renowned; he becomes intelligent.

yo modakasahasreṇa yajati sa vāñchitaphalamavāpnoti
Whoever worships with a thousand modakas (sweet dumplings, Gaṇeśa's favorite) obtains the fruit he desires.

yaḥ sājyasamidbhiryajati sa sarvaṃ labhate sa sarvaṃ labhate
Whoever worships with samidh (sacred fuel sticks) and ghee gains everything, gains everything.

## 14. Fruits of recitation, concluded

aṣṭau brāhmaṇānsamyaggrāhayitvā sūryavarcasvī bhavati
Whoever makes eight brahmins properly receive this becomes filled with the splendor of the sun.

sūryagrahe mahānadyāṃ pratimāsannidhau vā japtvā siddhamantro bhavati
Whoever recites it during a solar eclipse, at a great river, or in the presence of an image (of Gaṇapati) attains the full potency of this mantra.

mahāvighnātpramucyate
He is freed from great obstacles.

mahādoṣātpramucyate
He is freed from great faults.

mahāpāpātpramucyate
He is freed from great sin.

sa sarvavidbhavati sa sarvavidbhavati ya evaṃ veda
He becomes all-knowing, he becomes all-knowing, the one who knows this.

ityupaniṣat
Thus says the Upaniṣad.
